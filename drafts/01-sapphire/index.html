The distillation

Four decisions, and the last one is made by nose.

Macerate

Six of the seven botanicals sit in neutral grain spirit for three days at four degrees. Cold pulls the oils out slowly. Heat would be faster and would take the top notes with it.

Temperature4 °C
Duration72 hours

Vapour infuse

The seventh botanical never touches the liquid. Bladderwrack goes into a basket in the neck of the still and the vapour passes through it on the way out.

Still450 L copper
Run time7 hours

Cut

The heads come off early and the tails come off late, and both are judged by smell rather than by clock. It costs us roughly a fifth of every run.

Heart keptAbout 80%
Judged byNose

Rest

Three weeks in steel before it is cut with borehole water and bottled. Nothing is chill filtered, so a cold bottle will haze. That is the oils staying where they belong.

Resting21 days
FiltrationNone

The bottle is heavy on purpose.

Blue glass to keep light off the oils, a deep punt so it sits cold in the hand, and a label printed one colour on uncoated stock.

45.2% ABV

Bottled at strength and never watered down for softness. It holds its shape under tonic and under ice.

Keep the bottle in the freezer and the glass in there with it. At 45.2 percent it will not freeze, and the dilution you skip is the dilution you do not want.

  1. 50 mlAshmont
  2. 125 mlLight tonic, poured down a bar spoon
  3. FillCubed ice to the very top of the glass
  4. 1 wedgePink grapefruit, squeezed and dropped

Use more ice than feels reasonable. A half filled glass melts fast and turns the drink flat before you are halfway down it.

  1. 30 mlAshmont

  3. 30 mlSweet vermouth
  4. 1 cubeLarge clear ice, built in the glass

The bladderwrack sits well under Campari. It reads as salt at the back of the drink and pulls the bitterness forward instead of fighting it.

A headland that gets the weather first.

The distillery sits on nine acres of exposed coast. Two of the seven botanicals are picked within walking distance of the still, and the water is drawn from a borehole under the same ground.
