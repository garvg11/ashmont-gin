The idea

Most gin hides behind the botanicals. We took things out until there was nothing left to hide behind.

Máire Ó Dochartaigh Head Distiller

01 — The gin

Seven botanicals.
Each prepared alone.

Nothing goes into the still as a blend. Every botanical is macerated on its own schedule, then cut together at the last possible moment.

  1. 01

    Juniper

    Macedonian, two seasons dried. The spine.

    Macedonia
  2. 02

    Coriander

    Cracked, never milled. Citrus before spice.

    Bulgaria
  3. 03

    Lemon peel

    Peeled by hand the morning of the run.

    Sicily
  4. 04

    Angelica root

    Three years in the ground. Holds the middle.

    Poland
  5. 05

    Orris

    Six years rested. Used at a tenth of a gram.

    Tuscany
  6. 06

    Grains of paradise

    The heat that arrives last and stays longest.

    Ghana
  7. 07

    Meadowsweet

    Cut from the river margin below the distillery.

    Masuria

03 — Mastery

Three ways, and
no wrong ones.

01

The Ashmont & Tonic

50 ml Ashmont, 125 ml Indian tonic, one wide ribbon of lemon. Build over a single large piece of ice. Stir twice, then stop.

Highball · 3 min

02

Cold Martini

60 ml Ashmont, 10 ml dry vermouth, stirred thirty seconds at minus eighteen. Lemon oil across the surface, peel discarded.

Coupe · 4 min

03

Meadow Sour

45 ml Ashmont, 20 ml lemon, 15 ml meadowsweet honey. Shaken hard, double strained, no garnish at all.

Nick & Nora · 5 min
